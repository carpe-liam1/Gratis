Gratis is a publication project based out of San Francisco.

The goal is against immediacy.

We house personal ideas, curiosities,

and understandings into a cohesive product.

It is a documentation of non-institutional thoughtful work.

A physical magazine will be released each season

compiling extensive projects.

We want to express and impress upon others

a desire to value non-immediate things and thoughts

and to act upon these sentiments continuously

and with considerable weight.
